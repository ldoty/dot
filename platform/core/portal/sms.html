Dot-y text messages

Personal reminders and assistant texts

Dot-y is a personal assistant run by Luke Doty for himself and members of his family. It sends text messages from (864) 568-4810.

Messages are personal: reminders from your to-do list, reminders about meetings and appointments, and answers or summaries in reply to questions you text in. Some messages include links. Dot-y never sends marketing or promotional messages.

Example messages:

  • “Taking out the trash is on your todo list”
  • “Don’t forget you have a meeting with Jon today”
  • “Looks like you took a note on that meeting - here is the summary: …”
