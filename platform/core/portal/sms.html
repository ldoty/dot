Dot-y text messages

Your family assistant, by text

Dot-y is a family assistant app that helps a household keep track of to-dos, appointments and everyday questions. Dot-y is operated by Luke Doty and sends text messages from (864) 568-4810.

Messages are personal: reminders from your to-do list, reminders about meetings and appointments, and answers or summaries in reply to questions you text in. Dot-y never sends marketing or promotional messages.

Example messages:

  • “Taking out the trash is on your todo list”
  • “Don’t forget you have a meeting with Jon today”
  • “Looks like you took a note on that meeting - here is the summary: …”

Join Dot-y

Dot-y is invitation only. If someone in your household has invited you, join here and we’ll email you a sign-in for Dot-y on the web.

Text messages are optional. You can use Dot-y without them: leave the box unchecked and the mobile number blank. You can also sign up for texts later.
