Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

This policy explains how Luke Doty (“we”) handles information for Dot-y, a personal reminder and assistant text messaging program for Luke Doty and members of his family, sent from (864) 568-4810. Sign-up is at dot-y.co/sms.

Information we collect

  • Your name and mobile number, which you give us when you sign up.
  • Your consent record: the date and time you agreed, the exact consent wording you agreed to, and the IP address and browser type of the device used to sign up.
  • Message content: the text messages Dot-y sends you and the messages you send to Dot-y, including any questions, reminders or notes you share.

How we use it

We use this information only to run Dot-y for you: to send the reminders and assistant messages you signed up for, to answer and summarize what you text in, to respond to HELP requests, to honor STOP requests, and to keep a record that you agreed to receive messages. We do not sell your information, and we never use it for marketing.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

To operate the program, your information is processed by the service providers that deliver and host it on our behalf: our text messaging provider (Twilio) and our hosting provider (Amazon Web Services). They may use it only to provide those services to us. We may also disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

  • Name, mobile number and consent record: kept while you are signed up, and for up to four years after you opt out, so we can show that messages were sent with your consent and make sure we never text you again after STOP.
  • Message content: kept for up to 12 months, then deleted.

Your choices and deleting your information

Reply STOP at any time to stop all messages. To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email contact@dot-y.co from any address and include your mobile number. We will delete your information within 30 days, except the minimal record of your number and opt-out that we need to keep honoring your STOP request.

Security

Your information is stored with Amazon Web Services in encrypted storage, and access is limited to Luke Doty.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above. Questions: contact@dot-y.co. See also the SMS Terms.
