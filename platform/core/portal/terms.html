The program

Dot-y is a personal reminder and assistant text messaging program operated by Luke Doty, sent from (864) 568-4810. It is limited to Luke Doty and members of his family, by invitation. Messages include reminders from your to-do list, meeting and appointment reminders, and answers or summaries in reply to questions you text in. Messages may contain links. Dot-y does not send marketing messages.

You join by signing up at dot-y.co/sms and checking the consent box.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies, based on your reminders and the conversations you start. Dot-y is free. Msg & data rates may apply, according to your mobile plan.

Stopping messages

Reply STOP to cancel at any time. You will receive one message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages after that. The keywords UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END and QUIT also work. To join again, sign up at dot-y.co/sms.
